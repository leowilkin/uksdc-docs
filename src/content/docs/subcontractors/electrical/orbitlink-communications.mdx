<Aside title="TL;DR">
Satellite and laser-relay communications network, at $2 million per year (Common Orbit) or $250 million per year (Long Way).
</Aside>

The Orbitlink network utilises satellites and laser relays to transmit communications and bulk data across the solar system. Two packages are available:

- **Common Orbit**, providing 50 GB/s up and 100 GB/s down for $2,000,000 per year for settlements on or around an established planetary orbit.
- **Long Way**, providing 100 MB/s up and 200 MB/s down for $250,000,000 per year for settlements located anywhere else.

Currently, the Orbitlink network is established around the Earth and Moon.
