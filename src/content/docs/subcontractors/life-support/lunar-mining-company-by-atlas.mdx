<Aside title="TL;DR">
Lunar-sourced consumables shipped anywhere in the solar system in pressurised CASSSCs: water $4/kg, hydrogen $5/kg, nitrogen $20/kg, oxygen $24/kg.
</Aside>

Operating from a collective of small polar settlements, The Lunar Mining Company by Atlas (LMCA) ships the following in pressurised CASSSCs to anywhere in the solar system:

| Resource | Cost   |
| -------- | ------ |
| Nitrogen | $20/kg |
| Hydrogen | $5/kg  |
| Oxygen   | $24/kg |
| Water    | $4/kg  |

They hope that in the future, greater lunar infrastructure will be able to bring these costs down.
