Shelter produces custom protective fabric products including portable emergency shelters and airbags which charge $500 per m³ of enclosed space.

They also provide spacesuits containing life support systems, and an interconnected Heads-Up Display System (HUDS), which are charged at $200,000 for a hard-shell spacesuit and $100,000 for a softshell spacesuit.
