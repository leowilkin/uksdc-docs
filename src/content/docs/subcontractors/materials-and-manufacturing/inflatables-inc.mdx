Inflatables Inc. provides custom spherical and cylindrical inflatable volumes for settlement development, and construction, priced at $2,000 per enclosed m³.

Inflatables Inc. recommends that inflatables are enclosed with a protective layer or shield, to minimise the risk of puncture during long term use.
