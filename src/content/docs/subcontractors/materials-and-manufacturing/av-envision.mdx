AV Envision supplies Envision 3D printing facilities which use proprietary magnetised-bed SLS technology to produce one-off metal parts, and imitation natural materials such as wood and leather. Printing facilities have a maximum bed volume of 1m³, with the installation of a complete facility costing $20,000,000.
