Sealant Solutions provides airtight coatings for porous surfaces such as rock, superadobe, and ceramics. These are typically sprayed onto the surface or applied by brush and take 480 hours to dry on average. Priced at $15 per litre, coatings have a density of ~2000 kg/m³ and require a recommended thickness of 0.5mm.

These coatings can also be used as a thicker foam to patch holes and defects to maintain a pressure differential of 1atm for 5 days, with an emergency 10L tank priced at $1000.
